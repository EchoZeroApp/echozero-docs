title: Earnings and payouts
description: How success fees and subscriptions pay developers, and how to claim your USDC.

Developers earn from subscriber success fees and agent subscriptions. There are **no trading fees**: subscribers are never charged per trade, and you are never paid per trade. Everything is settled and paid in **USDC**.

All routes require developer registration and API key authentication with HMAC signing. See [API keys and HMAC](/guides/api-keys-hmac).

## Fee economics

| Stream | Who pays | Rate | Split |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Success fee** | Subscriber, only on **new profit highs** | You set `marketplaceSuccessFeePercent` (**0-22%**, default 20) | You keep your full success fee percent. EchoZero adds a fixed **8%** platform fee on top. |
| **Subscription** | Subscriber, monthly | You set `marketplaceMonthlyPrice` | **80%** to you, **20%** platform |
| **Both** | Subscriber | Subscription + success fee | Each stream as above |

The most a subscriber can pay in success fees is **30%** of new profit (your 22% maximum plus EchoZero's 8%).

## How the success fee works

EchoZero tracks a **high-water mark** (HWM) for every subscriber on every agent they follow. The HWM is the subscriber's best cumulative realized profit on that agent, net of venue costs, adjusted for their deposits and withdrawals.

- A fee accrues only when realized profit moves **above** the HWM, and only on the amount above it.
- No fee is charged on losses or on principal, and none is charged while the subscriber is below their previous peak. Recovering a drawdown is free.
- Accrued fees are **settled weekly** from the subscriber's balance. If a balance cannot cover the fee at settlement, the rest stays owed and is collected at a later settlement (catch-up).
- Fees also settle immediately when a subscriber **withdraws**, **unsubscribes**, or reduces their allocation to the agent.

### Worked example: success fee

Your `marketplaceSuccessFeePercent` is **15%**. A subscriber's realized profit on your agent reaches **$2,000**, and their previous high-water mark was **$0**.

1. Fee base: $2,000 - $0 = **$2,000** of new profit
2. Your creator fee: $2,000 × 15% = **$300**
3. EchoZero platform fee: $2,000 × 8% = **$160**
4. The subscriber pays **$460** at the next weekly settlement. Their HWM is now $2,000.

The following week, the subscriber's realized profit drops to $1,500 and then recovers to $2,300. Only the **$300** above the $2,000 HWM is charged: $45 to you and $24 to EchoZero.

<Note>
Accrued but unsettled fees are visible in your earnings totals but are **not claimable** until they have been collected at settlement.
</Note>

### Worked example: subscription

You set `marketplaceMonthlyPrice: 29`. Ten subscribers pay for one month.

1. Gross: **$290**
2. Platform fee (20%): **$58**
3. Your share: **$232**, claimable as USDC

## Monetization models

Set these on agent create or update (`POST /api/v1/agents`, `PATCH /api/v1/agents/{id}`):

| Model (`marketplacePricingModel`) | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| `subscription` | `marketplaceMonthlyPrice` |
| `success-fee` | `marketplaceSuccessFeePercent` (0-22) |
| `both` | `marketplaceMonthlyPrice` + `marketplaceSuccessFeePercent` |
| `free` | No charge |

`marketplaceSuccessFeePercent` defaults to **20** when you do not send it. You can also set the percentage with `feeConfiguration.feePercentage`.

<Note>
The request fields are prefixed with `marketplace`. When you read an agent back, or browse public agents, the same values are returned as `pricingModel`, `monthlyPrice` and `successFeePercent`. Sending the read names on create or update does not set the price.
</Note>

Values above **22** for the success fee are rejected on create and update.

## Getting paid

Every payout lands as **USDC in your EchoZero wallet**. You claim it; nothing moves automatically.

| Stream | Becomes claimable | Claim path |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Success fees** | After the weekly settlement collects them | `POST /v1/developers/claims/agents/{agentId}` or `/claims/all` |
| **Subscriptions** | Once the payment has been converted to USDC | `POST /v1/developers/claim-subscription-earnings` |

Each weekly settlement also records a payout snapshot (gross, platform fee, net) that you can read from payout history.

## Key endpoints

### Earnings summary

```
GET /api/v1/developers/earnings
```

Returns (all amounts in USD, paid as USDC):

- `totals`, `byAgent`: success-fee totals, including fees accrued but not yet settled
- `byPeriod`: weekly payout snapshots
- `fromTrading.claimableUsd`: success fees collected and ready to claim
- `fromSubscriptions.claimableUsd`: subscription earnings ready to claim
- `totalClaimableUsd`: both combined
- `lifetimeClaimedUsd`, `fromInstantClaims`: what you have already claimed

Check this before claiming. The `totalClaimable` field is a deprecated alias of `totalClaimableUsd`.

### Payout history

```
GET /api/v1/developers/payouts
```

Weekly payout rows: gross amount, platform fee, net. The response is `data.items` plus `data.pagination`. See [API conventions](/guides/conventions#pagination).

### Claim success fees

```
GET  /api/v1/developers/claims/strategy-fees     # per-agent claimable summary
POST /api/v1/developers/claims/agents/{agentId}  # claim one agent
POST /api/v1/developers/claims/all               # claim all owned agents
```

Transfers collected success fees to your wallet as USDC. Each call is capped per agent. If more remains after a claim, call again to claim the rest. Check `GET /api/v1/developers/earnings` for what is still claimable.

Returns **404** if the agent is not found or not owned by the caller.

### Claim subscription earnings

```
POST /api/v1/developers/claim-subscription-earnings
```

Claims all claimable subscription earnings as USDC into your EchoZero wallet. Returns the total USD claimed and the number of ledger rows updated.

Returns **400** when there is nothing to claim. Call `GET /earnings` first to check `fromSubscriptions.claimableUsd`.

<Warning>
**Stripe Connect payouts are retired.** Subscribers still pay subscriptions by card, but your share is paid as USDC through the claim endpoint above. The retired routes under `/api/developers/stripe-connect/*` (no `/v1`) return **410 Gone**. You do not need to onboard with Stripe.
</Warning>

## Typical workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register as a developer">
    Complete developer registration in the [Dev Portal](https://devportal.echozero.app) or via `POST /api/developers/register`. That route has no `/v1` and needs an authenticated user or API key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Price your agent">
    Set `marketplacePricingModel`, and `marketplaceSuccessFeePercent` (0-22) and/or `marketplaceMonthlyPrice`, when you create or update the agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request a listing">
    Send `isPublic: true` to ask for a marketplace listing. Staff review it. See [Agent lifecycle](/guides/agent-lifecycle).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor earnings">
    Poll `GET /v1/developers/earnings` for claimable balances and `GET /v1/developers/payouts` for weekly settlement history.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim balances">
    - Success fees: `POST /claims/agents/{agentId}` or `POST /claims/all`
    - Subscriptions: `POST /claim-subscription-earnings`
  </Step>
</Steps>
