- **Agent.** A trading strategy built by a creator. When you subscribe, it trades for you using the amount you set. See [Subscribing to an agent](/help/subscribing).
- **Allocation.** The USDC you set aside for one agent. The agent only trades with this amount.
- **All-time high.** The highest value your money with an agent has reached. Success fees only apply to profit above it. See [Fees](/help/fees).
- **Alert mode.** The agent asks you to accept each trade before it opens.
- **Full mode.** The agent trades automatically.
- **Liq price (liquidation price).** On a leveraged trade, the price at which the position is closed automatically. See [Leverage and liquidation](/help/leverage).
- **Leverage.** Trading a bigger position than the money committed, so gains and losses are multiplied. Shown as 5x, 10x and so on.
- **Long and short.** Long profits if the price rises. Short profits if it falls.
- **Max drawdown.** The biggest drop from a peak before recovering.
- **P&L.** Profit and loss. Green is up, red is down.
- **Perps (perpetual futures).** Positions that track a token's price, can be long or short, and can use leverage. Traded on Hyperliquid.
- **Rakeback.** Part of the platform fee you paid, returned to you based on your tier. See [Rewards](/help/rewards).
- **Spot.** Buying and selling the token itself, with no leverage. Traded on Solana through Jupiter.
- **Stop loss and take profit.** Prices at which a trade closes automatically, to limit a loss or lock in a gain.
- **Success fee.** The only fee on your trading results, charged on new profit only.
- **USDC.** A digital dollar. Your EchoZero balance is held in USDC on Solana.
- **Virtual mode.** Practice trading with paper funds. Pink means virtual.
- **Win rate.** The share of trades that closed in profit.
- **XP.** Points you earn by using EchoZero. XP sets your tier.
